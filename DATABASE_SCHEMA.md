# Cấu trúc Cơ sở dữ liệu (Database Schema)

Dự án này sử dụng kiến trúc Microservices. Mỗi service định nghĩa các model dữ liệu riêng bằng Django ORM. Dưới đây là danh sách chi tiết các bảng (Models) và các cột (Fields) phân chia theo từng service trong hệ thống.

## 1. Identity Service (Dịch vụ Định danh & Xác thực)
* **User**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `email` (EmailField)
  * `phone_number` (CharField)
  * `is_active` (BooleanField)
  * `is_staff` (BooleanField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `updated_at` (DateTimeField)
* **Role**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `name` (CharField)
  * `description` (TextField)
* **UserRole**
  * `user` (ForeignKey)
  * `role` (ForeignKey)

## 2. Patient Service (Dịch vụ Quản lý Bệnh nhân)
* **Patient**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `user_id` (UUIDField) - Liên kết với Identity Service
  * `first_name` (CharField)
  * `last_name` (CharField)
  * `date_of_birth` (DateField)
  * `gender` (CharField)
  * `blood_type` (CharField)
  * `address` (TextField)
  * `emergency_contact_name` (CharField)
  * `emergency_contact_phone` (CharField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `updated_at` (DateTimeField)
* **Allergy**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `patient` (ForeignKey)
  * `allergen` (CharField)
  * `severity` (CharField)
  * `reaction` (TextField)
  * `noted_date` (DateField)
* **MedicalCondition**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `patient` (ForeignKey)
  * `condition_name` (CharField)
  * `diagnosis_date` (DateField)
  * `status` (CharField)
  * `notes` (TextField)
* **CurrentMedication**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `patient` (ForeignKey)
  * `medication_name` (CharField)
  * `dosage` (CharField)
  * `frequency` (CharField)
  * `start_date` (DateField)
  * `end_date` (DateField)
  * `prescribed_by` (CharField)
  * `note` (TextField)
  * `is_active` (BooleanField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `updated_at` (DateTimeField)
* **Consent**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `patient` (ForeignKey)
  * `consent_type` (CharField)
  * `granted_to_user_id` (UUIDField)
  * `description` (TextField)
  * `is_active` (BooleanField)
  * `granted_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `revoked_at` (DateTimeField)

## 3. Provider Service (Dịch vụ Bác sĩ/Nhà cung cấp dịch vụ)
* **Specialty**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `name` (CharField)
  * `description` (TextField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
* **Clinic**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `name` (CharField)
  * `address` (TextField)
  * `contact_number` (CharField)
  * `description` (TextField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
* **Provider**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `user_id` (UUIDField) - Liên kết với Identity Service
  * `first_name` (CharField)
  * `last_name` (CharField)
  * `bio` (TextField)
  * `location` (CharField)
  * `clinic` (ForeignKey)
  * `rating` (DecimalField)
  * `status` (CharField)
  * `verification_notes` (TextField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `updated_at` (DateTimeField)
* **DoctorLicense**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `provider` (ForeignKey)
  * `license_number` (CharField)
  * `issue_date` (DateField)
  * `expiry_date` (DateField)
  * `document_url` (CharField)
  * `is_verified` (BooleanField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
* **ProviderService**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `provider` (ForeignKey)
  * `specialty` (ForeignKey)
  * `service_name` (CharField)
  * `price` (DecimalField)
  * `duration_minutes` (IntegerField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `updated_at` (DateTimeField)

## 4. Appointment Service (Dịch vụ Lịch hẹn)
* **AppointmentSlot**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `provider_id` (UUIDField)
  * `start_time` (DateTimeField)
  * `end_time` (DateTimeField)
  * `is_booked` (BooleanField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
* **Appointment**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `patient_id` (UUIDField)
  * `provider_id` (UUIDField)
  * `slot` (ForeignKey)
  * `status` (CharField)
  * `appointment_type` (CharField)
  * `reason_for_visit` (TextField)
  * `notes` (TextField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `updated_at` (DateTimeField)

## 5. Medical Record Service (Dịch vụ Hồ sơ Bệnh án)
* **MedicalRecord**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `patient_id` (UUIDField)
  * `provider_id` (UUIDField)
  * `appointment_id` (UUIDField)
  * `diagnosis` (TextField)
  * `treatment_plan` (TextField)
  * `notes` (TextField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `updated_at` (DateTimeField)
* **MedicalDocument**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `record` (ForeignKey)
  * `document_type` (CharField)
  * `file_url` (CharField)
  * `description` (TextField)
  * `uploaded_at` (DateTimeField)
* **LabResult**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `record` (ForeignKey)
  * `test_name` (CharField)
  * `test_date` (DateField)
  * `performed_by` (CharField)
  * `notes` (TextField)
* **LabResultItem**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `lab_result` (ForeignKey)
  * `parameter_name` (CharField)
  * `value` (CharField)
  * `unit` (CharField)
  * `reference_range` (CharField)
  * `is_abnormal` (BooleanField)
* **Vitals**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `record` (ForeignKey)
  * `recorded_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `height_cm` (DecimalField)
  * `weight_kg` (DecimalField)
  * `blood_pressure_systolic` (IntegerField)
  * `blood_pressure_diastolic` (IntegerField)
  * `heart_rate` (IntegerField)
  * `temperature_celsius` (DecimalField)
  * `respiratory_rate` (IntegerField)

## 6. Consultation Service (Dịch vụ Khám & Tư vấn)
* **Consultation**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `appointment_id` (UUIDField)
  * `patient_id` (UUIDField)
  * `provider_id` (UUIDField)
  * `status` (CharField)
  * `start_time` (DateTimeField)
  * `end_time` (DateTimeField)
  * `video_room_url` (CharField)
  * `summary` (TextField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `updated_at` (DateTimeField)
* **ConsultationNote**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `consultation` (ForeignKey)
  * `note_text` (TextField)
  * `is_private` (BooleanField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
* **Prescription**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `consultation` (ForeignKey)
  * `medication_name` (CharField)
  * `dosage` (CharField)
  * `frequency` (CharField)
  * `duration_days` (IntegerField)
  * `instructions` (TextField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)

## 7. Insurance Service (Dịch vụ Bảo hiểm)
* **InsurancePolicy**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `patient_id` (UUIDField)
  * `provider_name` (CharField)
  * `policy_number` (CharField)
  * `group_number` (CharField)
  * `plan_type` (CharField)
  * `valid_from` (DateField)
  * `valid_until` (DateField)
  * `is_active` (BooleanField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
* **EligibilityCheck**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `policy` (ForeignKey)
  * `checked_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `checked_by_user_id` (UUIDField)
  * `status` (CharField)
  * `coverage_details` (JSONField)
  * `notes` (TextField)
* **Claim**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `policy` (ForeignKey)
  * `appointment_id` (UUIDField)
  * `amount_claimed` (DecimalField)
  * `amount_approved` (DecimalField)
  * `status` (CharField)
  * `submitted_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `processed_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `denial_reason` (TextField)

## 8. Subscription Service (Dịch vụ Gói đăng ký)
* **Plan**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `name` (CharField)
  * `code` (CharField)
  * `description` (TextField)
  * `price` (DecimalField)
  * `billing_cycle` (CharField)
  * `is_active` (BooleanField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `updated_at` (DateTimeField)
* **PlanEntitlement**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `plan` (ForeignKey)
  * `feature_code` (CharField)
  * `value` (CharField)
  * `description` (TextField)
* **Subscription**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `patient_id` (UUIDField)
  * `plan` (ForeignKey)
  * `status` (CharField)
  * `current_period_start` (DateTimeField)
  * `current_period_end` (DateTimeField)
  * `cancel_at_period_end` (BooleanField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `updated_at` (DateTimeField)
* **SubscriptionUsage**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `subscription` (ForeignKey)
  * `entitlement` (ForeignKey)
  * `usage_limit` (IntegerField)
  * `current_usage` (IntegerField)
  * `reset_at` (DateTimeField)

## 9. Notification Service (Dịch vụ Thông báo)
* **NotificationTemplate**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `name` (CharField)
  * `type` (CharField)
  * `subject_template` (CharField)
  * `body_template` (TextField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
* **NotificationJob**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `recipient_id` (UUIDField)
  * `recipient_email` (EmailField)
  * `recipient_phone` (CharField)
  * `template` (ForeignKey)
  * `context_data` (JSONField)
  * `status` (CharField)
  * `scheduled_for` (DateTimeField)
  * `sent_at` (DateTimeField)
  * `error_message` (TextField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)
* **DeliveryLog**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `job` (ForeignKey)
  * `channel` (CharField)
  * `provider_response` (TextField)
  * `status` (CharField)
  * `created_at` (DateTimeField)

## 10. Audit Service (Dịch vụ Nhật ký / Audit)
* **AuditLog**
  * `id` (UUIDField)
  * `user_id` (UUIDField)
  * `service_name` (CharField)
  * `action` (CharField)
  * `resource_type` (CharField)
  * `resource_id` (CharField)
  * `details` (JSONField)
  * `ip_address` (GenericIPAddressField)
  * `timestamp` (DateTimeField)
